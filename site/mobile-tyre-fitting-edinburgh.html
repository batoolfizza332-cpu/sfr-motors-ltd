Driving In Edinburgh

Tyre Help Around The City And The Bypass

Edinburgh is a big city to get a flat in. The A720 City Bypass curves around its southern and western side for about 13 miles, from the A1 at Old Craighall to the A8 at Gogar, with major junctions at Sheriffhall, Straiton, Lothianburn, Calder and Hermiston Gait, where the M8 joins it. Inside the bypass, permit parking zones (now including areas such as Leith), narrow tenement streets and the city-centre Low Emission Zone, enforced since June 2024, can all make a garage trip on a damaged tyre slower and more awkward than it should be.

So we come to you instead. We can usually fit and repair tyres on residential streets, in workplace car parks and, where the site allows it, at the retail parks around the edge of the city, such as Fort Kinnaird beside the A1, Hermiston Gait off the bypass and the Gyle Centre in South Gyle. Send us your location and tyre size and we'll confirm a time; we're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are in the city and how busy we are.

Common Situations We Help With

Edinburgh Tyre Problems We Can Help With

On-Street Parking, No Driveway

In tenement areas such as Leith or Marchmont, plenty of cars live on the street. A slow puncture overnight doesn't have to mean a trip across town; in most cases we can do the work at the kerb where the car is already parked.

A Flat On The Bypass Commute

A tyre warning on the A720 on the way to Edinburgh Park or the airport. Leave at the next junction if you safely can — Gogar serves South Gyle and Edinburgh Park — then park up and send us your location so the rest of the day isn't lost to it.

Back To The Park & Ride

The city has park and ride sites at Ingliston and Hermiston in the west, Sheriffhall and Straiton in the south, and Newcraighall and Wallyford in the east. If you return from a day in town to a soft tyre, tell us which site you're at and roughly where the car is, and we'll check we can work there before we set off.

Edinburgh Neighbourhoods

Across The City, Not Just The Centre

We attend callouts in neighbourhoods right across Edinburgh, from Leith and Portobello near the coast to Corstorphine and the Gyle in the west and Morningside in the south, as well as business areas such as Edinburgh Park and the roads out towards the airport. Coverage varies with the day and how busy we are, so send your postcode or location and we'll confirm before anything is booked.

FAQs

Edinburgh Tyre Fitting Questions

Do you cover the whole of Edinburgh?

We cover homes, workplaces and car parks across the city, both inside and around the City Bypass. Send your location and we'll confirm before setting off.

Can you fit tyres if I only have on-street parking?

In most cases, yes. Tell us the street and whether there's room beside the car to work safely, and we'll confirm before we come out. If you're in a permit zone, make sure the car is legally parked.

Can you help if I get a flat on the City Bypass?

Yes. If the car can be driven safely, leave at the next junction and stop somewhere safe, then send us the junction name or a landmark, such as Hermiston Gait, Straiton or Sheriffhall, so we can find you.

Can you come to my car at a park and ride or retail park?

Usually, yes, where the site allows it and it's safe to work on the car. Tell us the site name, for example Ingliston, Hermiston or Fort Kinnaird, and which part of the car park you're in, and we'll confirm before we set off.

How quickly can you reach me in Edinburgh?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are in the city and how busy we are. We'll confirm a time before we leave.

Do you cover business areas like Edinburgh Park or out towards the airport?

Yes, we attend business parks and commercial addresses, including Edinburgh Park and the area around the airport. For vans or several company vehicles, our van & trade tyres page has more detail.

Tyre Trouble In Edinburgh?

Send us your Edinburgh postcode, tyre size and what's happened, and we'll reply with a price and an arrival time.
