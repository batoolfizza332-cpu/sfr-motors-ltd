Airdrie, North Lanarkshire

A Mobile Tyre Fitter For Airdrie's Busy Crossroads

Airdrie sits on raised ground roughly 12 miles east of Glasgow, where two important roads meet. The A89, the old route between Glasgow and Edinburgh, runs through Coatbridge, Airdrie and Plains on its way to Bathgate. The A73 crosses it in the town and heads south through Chapelhall to meet the M8 at Newhouse (junction 6). A lot of tyre trouble in Airdrie happens on one of those two roads or in the streets that branch off them.

Coming out from Bathgate, the A89 is our direct way into town. If a tyre goes flat at home, outside University Hospital Monklands on Monkscourt Avenue, or at a unit in Brownsburn Industrial Estate south of the town centre, there's no need to crawl to a garage on a damaged tyre. Send us your location and tyre size, and we'll confirm the tyre and a time before we leave. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are.

Common Situations We Help With

Real Situations Around Airdrie

A Flat On The A89 Through Town

The A89 carries traffic between Coatbridge, Airdrie and Plains all day. If a tyre goes on that stretch, turn into a side street or car park where it's safe, then send us a map pin. We bring the tyre to you, so you're not driving on the rim.

Stuck In The Hospital Car Park

Coming out of a long shift or a visit at University Hospital Monklands to find a flat is miserable. Tell us which car park you're in, and if the car can be worked on safely where it stands, we can repair or change the tyre there — we'll confirm before we set off.

Coming Off The M8 At Newhouse

Many drivers heading for Airdrie leave the M8 around Newhouse and follow the A73 up through Chapelhall. If a slow puncture shows itself on the way, stop somewhere safe off the main road and call us – a motorway hard shoulder is no place to change a wheel yourself.

FAQs

Questions From Airdrie Drivers

Which parts of Airdrie do you come out to?

The whole town, plus the villages that border it, such as Glenmavis, Chapelhall, Calderbank and Plains. Send us your street name or a map pin and we'll confirm cover before we set off.

How long will it take you to reach Airdrie?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes. It depends on where in Airdrie you are and how busy we are, and we'll confirm a time before we leave.

Can you help if I'm stuck on the A89, A73 or M8?

Yes. Get somewhere safe first – a lay-by, side street or car park rather than a live lane – then tell us exactly where you are and we'll confirm we can attend.

Will you come out at night or at the weekend?

Yes. We're open 24/7, so nights, weekends and early mornings are all covered.

How do you know you'll have my tyre?

Tell us the size printed on the tyre's sidewall (for example 205/55 R16) when you get in touch. We check what's available and confirm before setting off, so there's no wasted trip.

Do you look after vans for Airdrie businesses?

Yes, we fit and repair van tyres for traders and firms around Airdrie, including on industrial estates such as Brownsburn. Tell us the vehicle and tyre size and we'll confirm.
