Professional Locking Wheel Nut Removal

Locked Out Of Your Own Wheel?

Locking wheel nuts are meant to protect your alloys — until the key goes missing, snaps, or the nut itself seizes up, and suddenly you can't get your wheel off at all. Our fitters carry specialist extraction tools and come to your home, workplace or roadside location to assess the nut and remove it where it's safely possible to do so. Every locking nut is a little different, so we won't promise a fix before we've had a proper look — but it's the problem we're called out for most often, and our fitters have the tools and experience to handle it. Lost your key entirely? See our guide on what to do if you've lost your locking wheel nut key.

FAQs

Locking Wheel Nut Removal Questions

Can you remove any locking wheel nut?

We carry specialist tools to remove most locking wheel nuts on-site. Some, particularly heavily corroded or non-standard types, may need more time or equipment than can be resolved in one visit — our fitter will assess yours and let you know before starting any work.

Will removing a locking wheel nut damage my alloy wheels?

Our fitters use specialist extraction tools designed to minimise the risk of damage. As with any stuck or seized fastener, there's no way to fully guarantee zero risk, but care and the right equipment give the best chance of a clean removal.

What if I've lost my locking wheel nut key completely?

That's one of the most common reasons customers contact us. Let us know when you get in touch, and our fitter will bring the tools needed to remove a locking nut without the original key.

How long does locking wheel nut removal take?

Most jobs are completed within the same visit, though a heavily seized or corroded nut can take longer. Your fitter will give you a better idea once they've had a look.

Do I need a new locking wheel nut afterwards?

Once the old one is removed, we'd generally recommend fitting a replacement set with a new key so you're not left in the same position again — your fitter can talk you through the options.
