FAQs

Mobile Tyre Repair Questions

Can every punctured tyre be repaired?

Not always. Our fitter checks the position and size of the damage against standard safety guidance first. If it's a safe, repairable puncture we'll fix it on the spot; if not, we'll talk you through a replacement instead.

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. SFR Motors Ltd is a mobile service — we bring the repair to your home, workplace or roadside across Bathgate, Edinburgh and West Lothian.

How long does a mobile tyre repair take?

Most repairs are straightforward once our fitter is with you. Exact timing depends on the damage and your vehicle, so call us with the details for an honest estimate.

What if the tyre can't be repaired?

If the damage isn't safe to repair, we'll explain why and offer a replacement tyre fitted there and then, so you're not left waiting.

How quickly can you get to me?

Usually within 40–60 minutes, 24/7.
