FAQs

Mobile Puncture Repair Questions

Can every puncture be repaired?

Not always. A puncture in the central tread area can usually be repaired safely, but sidewall damage, large cuts or a previous repair too close by will usually mean the tyre needs replacing instead. Our fitter can assess this on-site.

Is it safe to drive on a punctured tyre?

Driving any distance on a flat or badly punctured tyre risks further damage to the tyre and wheel. If you can stop somewhere safe, it's best to wait there for us rather than continue driving.

How do I know if my puncture can be fixed on-site?

You don't need to know in advance — describe what's happened when you contact us, and our fitter will inspect the tyre on arrival to confirm whether it's repairable or needs replacing.

What should I do if I get a flat tyre?

Pull over somewhere safe as soon as you reasonably can, put your hazard lights on, and contact us with your location. Avoid continuing to drive on a flat tyre if at all possible.

How long does a mobile puncture repair take?

Most on-site puncture repairs are completed within the same visit, usually faster than a trip to a garage and back. Exact timing depends on where the tyre is punctured and how accessible your location is.
