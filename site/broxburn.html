Broxburn, West Lothian

Between The A89, The Union Canal And The Bings

Broxburn is a West Lothian town of around 16,000 people that runs straight into neighbouring Uphall. The A899 passes through the middle as East and West Main Street, while the A89 skirts the southern edge. The Union Canal threads through the town, and to the north-east the old shale bings, Greendykes being the largest in West Lothian, rise behind East Mains Industrial Estate.

Motorway links are close by: junction 3A of the M8 connects with the A89 for Broxburn and Bathgate, and the A89 also meets junction 1 of the M9 at Newbridge. Edinburgh Airport is about five miles away. Wherever the tyre lets you down, whether that's a works car park at East Mains or Greendykes, a side street off Main Street or the A89 itself, call or WhatsApp us. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are, and we confirm a time before setting off.
