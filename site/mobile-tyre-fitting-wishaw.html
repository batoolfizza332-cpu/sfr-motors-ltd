Wishaw, North Lanarkshire

Tyres Sorted Where You Are In Wishaw

Wishaw is about 6 km from Motherwell, with Newmains only 2 km away. The A721 is the main road in, linking the town with Motherwell and, beyond it, junction 6 of the M74. On the Newmains side, the A71, A73 and A722 meet at the Newmains roundabout, giving routes towards Edinburgh, Lanark and the M8. Main Street is the town's shopping area, and University Hospital Wishaw on Netherton Street runs a 24-hour A&E, so there are cars on the move here at every hour.

If a tyre lets you down at home in Pather or Waterloo, in the park and ride beside Wishaw station off Station Road, or outside the hospital, we bring the tyre, tools and wheel balancing equipment to you. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are, and we confirm the tyre size and an arrival time before anyone sets off.

Situations We See Around Wishaw

A Soft Tyre At The Station Park And Ride

Commuters using the park and ride beside Wishaw station sometimes come back off the Glasgow or Edinburgh train to a tyre that's gone down. Tell us roughly where you're parked and we can sort it in the car park.

A Puncture After A Hospital Visit

University Hospital Wishaw is busy day and night. If you find a flat after an appointment or a shift, tell us which car park you're in; where it's safe to work on the car there, we'll come to you so you aren't driving home on a damaged tyre.

A Warning Light At The Newmains Roundabout

The A71, A73 and A722 all meet at the Newmains roundabout, and a tyre warning can pop up as you go round it. Turn off into a side street or car park where it's safe to stop, then send us your location.
