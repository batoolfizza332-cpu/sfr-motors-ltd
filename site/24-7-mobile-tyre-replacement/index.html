FAQs

Mobile Tyre Replacement Questions

What counts as a tyre emergency?

A flat or badly damaged tyre that leaves you unable to drive safely — whether that's at the roadside, in a car park, or outside your home — counts as an emergency to us. If you're stuck, get in touch. If it happens while you're driving, see our guide on tyre blowouts for the safest way to respond.

What if I don't know my exact location?

That's fine — a nearby landmark, road name, postcode, or a location pin shared from your phone's maps app all work. Our fitter will confirm details with you by phone on the way.

What's the difference between tyre replacement and a repair?

A repair fixes a suitable puncture in place; a replacement fits a new tyre because the old one is too worn, damaged, or not safely repairable. We'll assess your tyre on-site and tell you honestly which applies before doing any work.

Do you carry my tyre size on the van?

Our vans carry a range of common car and van tyre sizes and brands. Tell us your size or registration when you get in touch and we'll confirm availability before we set off.

Can you replace just one tyre, or do I need a full set?

A single worn or damaged tyre can usually be replaced on its own. We'll give you an honest recommendation rather than upselling a full set you don't need.

How quickly can you get a replacement to me?

It depends on your location and how busy we are at the time, but our vans are on the road across West Lothian and Edinburgh every day. Get in touch with your location and we'll give you an honest estimate.

Is emergency tyre replacement available at night?

Yes. SFR Motors Ltd operates 24 hours a day, so emergency replacement callouts are available at night as well as during the day.
