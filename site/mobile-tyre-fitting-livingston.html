Mobile Tyre Fitting In Livingston

Tyre Fitting Built Around Livingston's Layout

Livingston was designated a new town in 1962 and is now the largest town in West Lothian. It was planned as separate districts — Craigshill, Ladywell, Knightsridge, Dedridge, Murieston, Eliburn, Deans, Howden and others — joined by distributor roads and roundabouts, with the River Almond running through the middle. The M8 runs along the northern edge. Junction 3 feeds the A899 dual carriageway, which runs down the east side of town to meet the A71 in the south.

That layout means a flat tyre can leave you some distance from a garage even in the middle of town. We come to you instead: a driveway in Dedridge or Murieston, a space at The Centre or Livingston Designer Outlet just off junction 3, a unit on the Houstoun or Deans industrial estates, or the station car parks at Livingston North and Livingston South. With Edinburgh about 15 miles east, plenty of people here commute, so we know a flat first thing is a real problem. Whether it's a puncture to look at or a full replacement, tell us where you are and we'll confirm a time.
