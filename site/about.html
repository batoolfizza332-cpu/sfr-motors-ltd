About SFR Motors Ltd

Secure. Fast. Reliable. Mobile tyre services across Scotland.

SFR Motors Ltd provides professional mobile tyre fitting and repair, bringing expert tyre assistance directly to you — at home, at work, or wherever you've broken down at the roadside. No towing your car to a garage, no waiting in a queue: our fully equipped vans come to you, wherever you are in West Lothian, Edinburgh, Falkirk and the surrounding areas.

Who We Are

A Local Mobile Tyre Team You Can Trust

SFR Motors Ltd is a Bathgate-based mobile tyre fitting company built on one idea: tyre trouble shouldn't mean an hour lost driving to a garage and waiting around. Our experienced, fully equipped fitters come to you instead, with the tools and stock to sort most tyre problems in a single visit. It's the same trusted trade skill you'd expect from a good local garage — just brought straight to your driveway, car park or roadside.

What We Do

Mobile Tyre Services, Start To Finish
