Mobile Tyre Fitting In Bathgate

Tyre Help In The Town We Call Home

SFR Motors is based in Bathgate, so a job here usually means a short drive across town rather than a trip across the county. The town sits just north of the M8. Junction 3A, signed for Bathgate, brings you in on the A89 from the Livingston side, while junction 4 at Whitburn meets the A801 to the west. On the eastern edge the A7066 runs between the Boghall and Inchwood roundabouts, and the B792 crosses the motorway south towards Blackburn and climbs north along Torphichen Road towards the Bathgate Hills.

That gives us plenty of places we can meet you: the station car park on Edinburgh Road, where commuters leave cars for trains to Edinburgh and Glasgow; the free car park at Balbardie Park beside Xcite Bathgate; industrial estates such as Whiteside and Whitehill; or a driveway in Boghall or near the Steelyard in the town centre. Wherever your car, van or trailer is sitting, we bring the tyres, tools and wheel balancing equipment to it — no towing and no garage queue. Get in touch and we'll confirm your tyre size, availability and a time before we set off.

FAQs

Questions From Bathgate Drivers

Where in Bathgate are you based?

SFR Motors is based in Bathgate itself, so a callout anywhere in town, from Boghall to the streets around the Steelyard, usually starts close by. We come to you rather than you coming to us.

Do you cover areas near Bathgate too?

Yes. Livingston, Armadale, Whitburn and Blackburn are all close by and we regularly attend them. Tell us where you are and we'll confirm.

Can you come to my workplace or the station car park in Bathgate?

Yes. Workplaces, the industrial estates, the station car park on Edinburgh Road and other car parks around town are usually fine, as long as the vehicle is somewhere safe to work on and the site allows it. Tell us the exact spot when you get in touch and we'll confirm before we set off.

Do I need to bring my car to you?

No. Driving on a flat or damaged tyre can make things worse, so we come to wherever the vehicle is in Bathgate and do the work there.

What if I need help outside normal hours?

We're open 24/7, so call or WhatsApp at any hour and we'll tell you what we can do. For a roadside emergency, our emergency tyre change service is set up for exactly that.

Since you're based in Bathgate, is the response quicker here?

Often, yes, because we don't have to travel in from elsewhere first. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, but it depends on where you are and how busy we are, so we'll confirm a realistic time before setting off.

Need Tyre Help In Bathgate?

We're based right here. Call or WhatsApp with your location in Bathgate and what's happened, and we'll check the tyre size and availability and confirm a time before we leave.
