Addiewell, West Lothian

Tyre Help For Addiewell And Loganlea

Addiewell is a former mining village next to West Calder, made up of two districts, Addiebrownhill and Loganlea, with Stoneyburn close by. The village has its own stop on the Shotts line between Edinburgh and Glasgow, and HMP Addiewell stands on Station Road.

The B792 between West Calder and Blackburn runs just east of the village, with Addiewell Bing nature reserve beside it. These roads are quieter than the A71 through West Calder, which is good news until a tyre goes and a workshop is a drive away. We bring the tyres, tools and wheel balancing to your door instead.

Situations We Help With In Addiewell

A Flat Waiting At The Station

The small car park at Addiewell station only takes a handful of cars, so space around a parked car can be tight. If you step off a late train to find a soft tyre, send us your location and we'll work out a safe spot to sort it.

Addiewell station's car park only has around a dozen spaces, so if you've come off the train to a flat, tell us where you're parked and we'll work around the space.
