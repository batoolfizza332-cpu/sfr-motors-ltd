Kirkliston, West Of Edinburgh

A Village Hemmed In By Motorways And The Airport

Kirkliston is a village in the City of Edinburgh council area, built on raised ground just above a loop of the River Almond. Two roads cross at its centre: the B800, which runs north to south as Path Brae, the High Street, Station Road and Queensferry Road, and the B9080, which leaves as Main Street and Stirling Road. The old road east towards the city no longer runs through, because Edinburgh Airport now sits across its line.

That layout catches people out when a tyre goes. Junction 1A, where the M90 leaves the M9 for the Queensferry Crossing, is right on the western edge of the village, yet it has no slip roads into Kirkliston. The closest way on or off is junction 1 at Newbridge, about two miles south down the B800. So instead of nursing a soft tyre round to a garage, stop somewhere safe and call or WhatsApp us with your location and tyre size. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are, and we confirm a time before setting off.

Real Situations Around Kirkliston

Slow Puncture Coming Off The M90

You head south over the Queensferry Crossing and the steering starts to pull. There's no exit into Kirkliston at junction 1A, so leave the motorway at the first safe point, stop away from traffic and send us a map pin.

A Flat On The Morning Of A Flight

The tyre is down on the day you're due at the airport. Ring us as soon as you notice; we'll give you a realistic time so you can decide whether to wait or make other plans for the journey.

A Kerbed Tyre In The Old Village Streets

Questions From Kirkliston Drivers

Which motorway junction is closest to Kirkliston?

Junction 1 of the M9 at Newbridge, about two miles south via the B800. Junction 1A, where the M90 begins, has no slip road into the village. If you break down on the motorway, get to a safe place first, then tell us exactly where you are.

How soon can you get to Kirkliston?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are. We'll confirm a time before we set off.

Do you also cover Broxburn and Edinburgh?

Yes, we cover both. Send your location and we'll confirm.

Can you come out very early or late at night?

Yes, we're open 24/7, so an early shift or a late return from the airport is no problem.

Will you have my tyre size in stock?

Send us the size printed on the tyre sidewall, for example 205/55 R16, and we'll check availability before we leave.

Do you fit van tyres in Kirkliston?

Yes, we fit and repair van tyres for local traders and businesses. Tell us the van and tyre size and we'll confirm.
