FAQs

Emergency Tyre Change Questions

What counts as a tyre emergency?

A flat or badly damaged tyre that leaves you unable to drive safely — whether that's at the roadside, in a car park, or outside your home — counts as an emergency to us. If you're stuck, get in touch.

How quickly can you get to me in an emergency?

It depends on your location and how busy we are at the time, but our vans are on the road across West Lothian and Edinburgh every day. Call us with your location and we'll give you an honest estimate.

Is emergency tyre assistance available at night?

Yes. SFR Motors Ltd operates 24 hours a day, so emergency callouts are available at night as well as during the day.

What if I don't know my exact location?

That's fine — a nearby landmark, road name, postcode, or a location pin shared from your phone's maps app all work. Our fitter will confirm details with you by phone on the way.

Do you carry replacement tyres on the van?

Our vans carry a range of common tyre sizes and brands. Tell us your tyre size when you call so we can confirm availability before we set off.

Stranded Right Now?

Don't wait it out. Call us and we'll get someone to you as fast as we can.
