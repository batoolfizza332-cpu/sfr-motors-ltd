FAQs

Mobile Trailer & Caravan Tyre Fitting Questions

Do caravan and trailer tyres need replacing more often than car tyres?

Often yes, because caravans and trailers can sit unused for long periods, and tyres degrade with age even without much mileage. It's worth having them checked regularly rather than judging purely by tread wear.

Can you fit tyres to a caravan that's in storage?

In most cases, yes — let us know the storage location when you get in touch and we'll confirm we can get access to fit or replace the tyres there.

Do you carry specialist caravan and trailer tyre sizes?

Caravan and trailer tyres often differ from standard car sizes. Tell us your size and requirements when you get in touch and we'll confirm availability before we come out.

What if my caravan or trailer has a puncture?

We can assess the puncture on-site and carry out a suitable repair where it's safe to do so, or fit a replacement if the tyre isn't repairable.

Can you check tyre condition without a full replacement?

Yes. If you're not sure whether your caravan or trailer tyres need attention, we can carry out a check and give you honest advice before any work goes ahead.
