Getting Around Linlithgow

A Loch-Side Town Between The M9 And The Union Canal

Linlithgow is a historic West Lothian town roughly 20 miles west of Edinburgh, with the palace and Linlithgow Loch at its centre and the M9 skirting its northern edge. The A803 is the main east–west road and becomes the High Street as it passes through, while the A706 is the main north–south road and leads up to Bo’ness. Across the southern half of town, the railway and the Union Canal both cut through, and there are only a few places to cross them, so queues build at those points when it's busy.

That layout matters when a tyre fails. Nursing a damaged tyre across town to a garage means sitting in those queues on a wheel that may not take it. We'd rather come to the car. Send us your location, the tyre size printed on the sidewall and a photo if you can, and we'll check we have the right tyre on board before setting off. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are at the time.

Common Situations We Help With

Real Situations Around Linlithgow

A Soft Tyre On The M9

Know your junctions. Heading north from Edinburgh you can leave at Junction 3 for Linlithgow, but travelling south towards Edinburgh there's no exit at Junction 3, so the way off is Junction 4 at Polmont, or Junction 2 near Philpstoun if you've already passed it. If the tyre won't make it, stop somewhere safe and call us.

Back To A Flat At The Station

Linlithgow station, on the Edinburgh–Glasgow line via Falkirk, has a car park of around 96 spaces. If you step off an evening train to find a flat, tell us which part of the car park you're in and, where it's safe and allowed to work on the car there, we'll come to it, so you're not changing a wheel in the dark.

A Day Out At The Palace Or Beecraigs

A visit to Linlithgow Palace and the loch, or a walk at Beecraigs Country Park in the Bathgate Hills just outside town, can end with a soft tyre in the car park. Let us know which car park you're in, and mention it if your locking wheel nut key is missing.

Linlithgow & Neighbouring Places

Linlithgow Bridge, Philpstoun And Beyond

Our Linlithgow callouts include Linlithgow Bridge on the western side of town, near the River Avon, and Philpstoun, a village about 2 miles to the east between the Union Canal and the railway. We also cover Bathgate and Livingston elsewhere in West Lothian, and Bo’ness just to the north on the Forth. How far we can reach depends on the day and our workload, so if you are unsure about your street, send your location and we'll tell you before anything is booked.

FAQs

Linlithgow Tyre Questions Answered

Which M9 junction should I use for Linlithgow?

Coming from Edinburgh, leave at Junction 3. Travelling towards Edinburgh there is no exit at Junction 3, so use Junction 4 at Polmont, or Junction 2 near Philpstoun if you have passed it. If the tyre won't get you that far, stop somewhere safe and call us instead.

How long will it take you to reach me in Linlithgow?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, depending on where you are and how busy we are. We confirm a time with you before we set off.

Can you repair a puncture instead of replacing the tyre?

Often, yes. If the damage is in the repairable part of the tread and the tyre hasn't been driven on while flat, a repair may be possible. We inspect it at your car and tell you honestly if a new tyre is the safer choice.

Do you come out at night or at weekends?

Yes. We're open 24/7, so a flat before an early train from Linlithgow or after a late shift is fine to call about.

What do you need from me before you set off?

Your location, your tyre size (printed on the sidewall, for example 205/55 R16) and a quick description of the problem. That lets us confirm we have a suitable tyre with us before we travel.

Can you fit tyres on a van in Linlithgow?

Yes. We fit and repair van tyres with the correct load rating at your home, yard or work site in and around Linlithgow.

Do you cover Linlithgow Bridge and Philpstoun?

Yes, both are part of our Linlithgow callouts, 24/7. Call 0131 202 0289 or WhatsApp your location to 07448 427154.

Tyre Trouble In Linlithgow?

Tell us where you are in Linlithgow, your tyre size and what's happened, and we'll confirm the tyre and an arrival time before we leave.
