Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information SFR Motors Ltd collects through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us using the details in section 1.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

This website is operated by SFR Motors Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15819240). SFR Motors Ltd is the business responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

  • Service location: Bathgate, West Lothian. We are a mobile tyre fitting service and come to you.
  • Telephone: 0131 202 0289
  • WhatsApp: 07448 427154
  • Email: info@sfrmotors.co.uk

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, book our mobile tyre fitting services, or contact us, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name and Contact Information: Phone number, email address, and service location/address.
  • Vehicle Details: Vehicle make, model, registration number, and tyre specifications.
  • Transaction Details: Details regarding the tyre fitting and replacement services you have requested.
  • Enquiry Details: What you tell us when you contact us by telephone, email or WhatsApp, including the number or email address you contact us from and the content of your message.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our mobile tyre fitting services.
  • Process your bookings and communicate with you regarding your service appointments.
  • Send you service updates, confirmations, or customer support responses.
  • Improve our website layout, performance, and user experience through analytics, where you have accepted analytics cookies.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Telephone, Email and WhatsApp Enquiries

You can contact us by telephone (0131 202 0289), by email (info@sfrmotors.co.uk) or on WhatsApp (07448 427154). We use the details you give us to respond to your enquiry, arrange and carry out the service you ask for, and keep records of it.

This website does not send your quote-form details to a server of ours. If you complete the quote form and choose to send it, your device opens WhatsApp with a message pre-filled with what you entered; nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is operated by a third party (WhatsApp, part of Meta). When you use WhatsApp to contact us, whether through our quote form, one of the WhatsApp links on this website, or by messaging us directly, the information you send is shared with that provider, and the provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to it. Please do not send more personal information than is needed for your enquiry.

5. Log Files & Analytics (Google Analytics)

SFR Motors follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

Google Analytics is optional and only runs with your consent. If you choose “Accept analytics”, we use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how this website is used — for example which pages are viewed and how often our telephone and WhatsApp contact links are used — so that we can improve the website and our mobile tyre services. If you choose “Reject analytics”, or you have not made a choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

While analytics is on, your browser connects to Google, so Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address and browser and device details, together with the cookie identifiers described below. When you click a telephone or WhatsApp link our own tracking records only that a click happened and where on the page it was, not any message. Depending on the measurement settings in our Google Analytics account, Google Analytics may also automatically record general interactions such as scrolling and clicks on links to other websites, including the address of the link clicked (for example our own WhatsApp contact link). We do not send your name, your own telephone number, your email address or anything you type into our quote form to Google Analytics, and Google signals and advertising personalisation are switched off for our Google Analytics set-up. This website does not currently use Google Ads conversion or remarketing tags.

Google's own explanation of how it uses information from sites that use its services is at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Cookies and Web Beacons

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store. This website uses the following:

  • Cookie preference (essential): sfr_consent, set by SFR Motors on this website. It remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies so that we do not ask again on every page. It contains only your choice (analytics granted or denied) and a version marker — no personal information. It is kept for up to 180 days, after which we ask again, unless you change your choice or delete it sooner. Because it is needed to respect your choice, it is set without asking.
  • Google Analytics 4 (optional, only after you accept): _ga, used to distinguish visitors, and _ga_B9TY4GMXYT, used to keep session state. Both are set on our domain by Google's script and are stored for up to 2 years (Google's default); some browsers shorten this.

Analytics data is sent to Google using small tracking requests (sometimes called web beacons), and only while analytics is on. You can change your mind at any time: choose “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page and select “Accept analytics” or “Reject analytics”. If you reject after previously accepting, we stop Google Analytics, remove the Google Analytics cookies that this website set, and reload the page. You can also delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

The fonts on this website are hosted on the website itself and set no cookies. The Google Map on the Contact page is not loaded unless you choose to load it; if you do, Google may set its own cookies (see the section on third-party services below).

7. Third-Party Services: Google Maps, Fonts and WhatsApp

SFR Motors's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

Google Maps (only if you choose). The Contact page shows a button, “Load Google Map”. Until you press it, your browser makes no connection to Google for the map. If you press it, the map loads from Google, which receives your IP address and other technical information and may set cookies, under Google's own policy (linked in the analytics section above). The map shows the general Bathgate service area only. The “View Bathgate area on Google Maps” link opens Google Maps only if you click it.

Fonts. The Roboto typeface is hosted on this website. It is no longer loaded from Google Fonts, so displaying our pages does not send your IP address to Google for fonts.

WhatsApp. See the section on enquiries above: WhatsApp is operated by a third party and information you send through it is shared with that provider.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purposes it was collected for: dealing with your enquiry, providing and administering the services you request, keeping our business and accounting records, and meeting our legal obligations. We have not set fixed retention periods on this page. If you would like to know how long we keep particular information about you, please ask us using the contact details above. The cookie lifetimes that apply to this website are stated in the cookies section.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Under UK data protection law, and depending on the circumstances, you have rights over your personal information. In summary, you can:

  • ask us what personal information we hold about you and for a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to erase your information, or to restrict how we use it, in some situations;
  • object to us using your information where we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • ask for information you gave us to be provided in a portable format, where that applies;
  • withdraw a consent you have given at any time (for analytics cookies, use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page).

These rights are summarised here and do not apply in every situation. To make an enquiry about your information or your rights, contact us by email at info@sfrmotors.co.uk, by telephone on 0131 202 0289, or on WhatsApp on 07448 427154. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on a request. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. SFR Motors does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately, after they are posted on this page.

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, or want to make a privacy enquiry, contact SFR Motors Ltd by email at info@sfrmotors.co.uk, by telephone on 0131 202 0289, or on WhatsApp on 07448 427154. Our service location is Bathgate, West Lothian.
