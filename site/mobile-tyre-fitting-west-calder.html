West Calder, West Lothian

Tyre Trouble On The A71? We Come To You

West Calder lies on the A71, the main road from Edinburgh to Kilmarnock, a short drive west of Livingston. Heading east, the road passes Polbeth on its way towards Livingston; heading west, it carries on to Breich. The B792 leaves the village northwards towards Blackburn, passing Addiewell Bing nature reserve on the way.

The village has its own station on the Shotts line between Edinburgh and Glasgow, and the Five Sisters shale bings, a scheduled monument, stand out on the skyline to the north. For drivers the A71 is the road that matters: it carries through traffic all day, and a puncture there means finding somewhere safe to pull in. We'd rather come to you than have you limp to a garage on a damaged tyre.

West Calder Tyre Questions Answered

Do you cover the whole of West Calder?

Yes, from Hermand on the western edge to the eastern side towards Polbeth. Send us your address or a map pin and we'll confirm before we set off.

How soon can you get to West Calder?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes. It depends on your exact location and how busy we are, and we confirm a time before leaving.

I've got a flat on the A71 – can you help?

Yes. Move somewhere safe off the carriageway if you can, then call or WhatsApp with your location – a nearby junction or landmark helps – and we'll confirm we can attend.

Can you meet me at West Calder station?

Yes, tell us where in the car park you are. It's a small car park, so we'll check there's space around your car before starting.

What do you need to know about my tyres?

The size from the tyre sidewall and your vehicle details. We confirm availability before setting off.

Do you fit van tyres in West Calder?

Yes, we fit and repair van tyres for tradespeople and businesses in West Calder – tell us what you need and we'll confirm.
