Blackburn, West Lothian

A Village On The Almond, Between Bathgate And Livingston

Blackburn is a village a couple of miles south of Bathgate, built along the River Almond. The A705 runs through it, joining Whitburn in the west with Livingston in the east, where it meets the A899. The B792 Bathgate Road heads north out of the village towards Bathgate, and the M8 runs just to the north.

There's no railway station in Blackburn itself; the nearest is in Bathgate. So if a tyre fails, we come to your home, the school gates or the side of the A705 instead, and we're usually with you within 40–60 minutes. Please note we mean Blackburn in West Lothian, not the town of the same name in Lancashire.

When Blackburn Drivers Need Us

A Flat On The School Run

St Kentigern's Academy and Blackburn Primary are both in the village, so a flat at drop-off time is a real headache. Leave the car somewhere safe, get the children to class, and we'll meet you back at the car.

Trouble On The A705 Towards Livingston

The A705 links Blackburn with Livingston and Whitburn. If a tyre goes on that stretch, pull into a safe spot, put your hazards on and call; we'll ask for the nearest junction so we can find you.

Stuck At Home In Seafield Or East Whitburn

Woken up to a flat on the street outside your house in Seafield or East Whitburn? Stay put. We'll come to your door and fit or repair the tyre there.

Blackburn Questions We Get Asked

Is this Blackburn in Lancashire?

No. This page is for Blackburn in West Lothian, between Bathgate and Livingston. We don't work in Lancashire.

How fast can you reach Blackburn, West Lothian?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, day or night. Timing depends on your exact location and how busy we are, so we'll confirm before setting off.

Can you meet me near the school or at my work?

Yes, as long as the car is parked somewhere safe and legal where we can work around it. Send us the location and we'll confirm.

Do you cover Seafield and East Whitburn?

Yes, both sit right next to Blackburn. Tell us your address and we'll confirm before booking.

What if you don't have my tyre size?

Send us the size from the side of the tyre first. We'll check availability and tell you before we set off, so nobody makes a wasted trip.

Can you fit tyres on a van in Blackburn?

Yes. We fit and repair van tyres for local tradespeople, and we also handle cars, caravans and small trailers. Tell us what you drive and we'll confirm.
