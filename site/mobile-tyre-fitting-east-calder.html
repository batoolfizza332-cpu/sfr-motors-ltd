Tyre Help That Comes To You In East Calder

East Calder lies south-east of Livingston, about a mile east of Mid Calder, with the A71 bypassing the village to the south on its way towards Edinburgh. The B7015 forms the Main Street, and the village has grown with new housing at Calderwood and Raw Holdings. We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, day or night. A puncture at home, a warning light on the way to work, or a tyre that won't make it back from a walk at Almondell & Calderwood Country Park — we bring the tyres, tools and wheel balancing equipment to wherever you are.

East Calder Tyre Fitting Questions

Do you cover Kirknewton and Wilkieston too?

Yes, along with East Calder we attend Kirknewton, Wilkieston and Oakbank — let us know your location and we'll confirm before we set off.

How quickly can you get to me in East Calder?

We're usually with you within 40–60 minutes, 24 hours a day. It depends on where you are and how busy we are at the time, so call or WhatsApp us and we'll give you a time before we set off.

Can you come to me if I'm parked at Almondell & Calderwood Country Park?

Usually, yes, as long as the car is somewhere we can safely work on it, such as a car park, and the park's rules allow it. Send us the location and we'll confirm.
